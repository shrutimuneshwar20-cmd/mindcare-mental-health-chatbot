Evidence-Based Self-Care Recommendations

Stress Management

  • Deep breathing: Inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 6 seconds to trigger parasympathetic rest.
  • Short breaks: Take 5-minute pauses away from your desk every hour.
  • Task chunking: Prioritize 1 or 2 essential tasks instead of an impossible list.
  • Movement: A gentle 10-minute walk significantly reduces cortisol.

Managing Anxiety

  • Grounding: Touch physical surfaces (table, clothes, water) to exit mental spirals.
  • Moderate caffeine: High coffee or energy drink intake mimics panic sensations.
  • Verbalize worries: Talk to someone you trust; voice-sharing reduces threat perception.
  • Accept the wave: Anxiety peaks and naturally subsides within 15–20 minutes.

Rest & Sleep Hygiene

  • Screen curfew: Disconnect from phones/screens 45 minutes prior to sleep.
  • Consistent schedule: Wake up and sleep at consistent times daily.
  • Dark & cool space: Keep the room cooler; lower temperature facilitates melatonin release.
  • Worry dump: Write next day's to-dos in your journal so your brain rests.

Exam & Academic Pressure

  • Pomodoro Study: Study for 25 minutes, followed by 5 minutes of genuine relaxation.
  • Avoid all-nighters: Sleep deprivation impairs memory consolidation and exam recall.
  • Realistic pacing: Focus on understanding key concepts rather than memorizing blindly.
  • Perspective: Your exams measure preparation for a test, never your human dignity.
Project Mission

About MindCare AI

MindCare AI is an educational mental health awareness chatbot designed to provide supportive conversations, wellness information, and simple self-care techniques.

Never Diagnoses

MindCare AI never provides clinical diagnoses for depression, bipolar disorder, anxiety disorders, or any medical condition. Only licensed medical professionals can evaluate health.

No Prescriptions

MindCare AI never suggests medications, dosages, or adjustments. Any pharmaceutical inquiries should always be directed to a registered psychiatrist or physician.

Supports Professional Care

MindCare actively encourages reaching out to university counselors, psychologists, therapists, and doctors whenever distress feels prolonged or unmanageable.

Crisis Awareness

Equipped with proactive safety triggers to immediately surface verified regional emergency helplines if danger or self-harm is detected.

Medical & Psychological Disclaimer

This application provides general mental health awareness information and is not a substitute for professional medical or psychological care, clinical diagnosis, or emergency intervention. If you are experiencing intense distress, thoughts of self-harm, or are in immediate physical danger, please immediately contact emergency services or a verified crisis helpline.
